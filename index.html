Saved forecast accuracy & methodology

Accuracy = 100 × (1 − WAPE). WAPE = sum of absolute daily queue/case-type errors ÷ sum of actual reviews. It is unavailable when actual volume is zero; accuracy can be negative for very poor forecasts. Positive bias means overforecasting. No error cancellation across queues is used. Models are evaluated on the same final held-out days for series with enough common history.

Auto-select uses up to three earlier non-overlapping validation windows; the final holdout is not used to choose its model. Holt–Winters uses fixed α=0.2, β=0.05, γ=0.2 and weekly additive seasonality. No annual seasonality, prediction intervals or ARIMA fit is implied. Saved versions are session data unless included in a shared snapshot. Saved-version scores use only dates after the version was created.
